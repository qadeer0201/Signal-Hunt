RULES

SH-04
  1. 01Teams must decode each clue in sequence and submit answers on the web terminal.
  2. 02Teams must not skip stages or enter restricted campus areas.
  3. 03Work strictly with your registered team members (up to 5 per team).
  4. 04Do not tamper with physical clues or other teams' station materials.
  5. 05Do not use outside assistance or unauthorized AI help.
  6. 06Find physical locations from the riddles. Stay inside organizer-approved event areas and follow volunteer directions.
  7. 07After login, remain in browser fullscreen. A detected fullscreen exit eliminates the team; only an organizer can reinstate it.
  8. 08Your level timer keeps running while eliminated. Organizer reinstatement does not reset or refund elapsed time.
  9. 09Use in-app navigation between Home, Mission, Knowledge, Campus Map, Rules and Security. Keep fullscreen active during gameplay.
  10. 10Submitting an envelope verification code records that level. The physical volunteer decides the official finishing order.
  11. 11Keep team login codes and envelope verification codes private.

EVENT INTEGRITY POLICY

STAY WITHIN THE MISSION

After login, keep Signal Hunt in browser fullscreen for the duration of the game. The allowed website pages remain available through in-app navigation.

During active gameplay, a detected fullscreen exit is reported to the backend and eliminates the team. The backend records the violation, the level timer continues, and an organizer can reinstate the team without restoring elapsed time. Browser and operating-system controls cannot be made tamper-proof.
